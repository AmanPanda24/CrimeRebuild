How it works

From scattered files to one clear story

Upload evidence from any forensic tool. CrimeRebuild converts everything to one format, puts every event on a single timeline, flags suspicious behaviour and writes the report. Nothing is guessed: every finding points back to the events behind it.

How the risk score works

Each alert adds points by severity. The total is capped at 100. The score is a triage aid, not a verdict, and the alert list explains every point.

{% for lv, pts in [('low', 10), ('medium', 20), ('high', 35), ('critical', 50)] %}
+{{ pts }}{{ lv }} alert
{% endfor %}
Low0 to 24
Medium25 to 49
High50 to 74
Critical75 to 100

Example: USB exfiltration (critical, 50) plus deletion after copy (high, 35) = 85, Critical.

Evidence stays intact

  • Originals are copied read-only and never modified.
  • SHA-256 is recorded at intake. Use Verify integrity to re-check at any time.
  • Every step is written to the chain-of-custody log.
  • All analysis runs on your own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Good to know

  • Times without a time zone are treated as UTC.
  • Detection is rule-based: it finds known patterns, not unknown attacks.
  • Thresholds can be tuned in Detection settings.
  • An academic tool, not court-admissible forensic software.
